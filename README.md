Words That Think: The Adventures of Tom Sawyer
An interactive vocabulary learning app for The Adventures of Tom Sawyer by Mark Twain. Master the language of childhood mischief and adventure with bilingual definitions, original book quotes, example sentences, synonyms, antonyms, and self‑quizzes.

✨ Features
	•	🗂️ Grouped Learning – 257 words organized into 26 groups of 10 for manageable study sessions
	•	📖 Original Quotes – See exactly how Mark Twain used each word in the novel
	•	🌏 Bilingual – English definitions + Chinese translations for all content
	•	🔤 Pronunciation – IPA pronunciation guide for every word
	•	🔄 Synonyms & Antonyms – Expand your vocabulary with related words for all 257 entries
	•	📝 My Sentences – Write and save your own sentences using each word
	•	🎮 Two Quiz Modes
	◦	Group Quiz – Test yourself on the current group
	◦	Starred Words Quiz – Quiz yourself only on words you've starred
	•	⭐ Star System – Mark important words for later review
	•	🔊 Text‑to‑Speech – Hear the correct pronunciation
	•	🖨️ Printable – Generate a clean, printer‑friendly word list

🚀 Quick Start
Online Version (Recommended)
Just visit: 👉 https://dwang055.github.io/words_that_think_tom_sawyer/
Offline / Local Use
	1	Download both files from this repository:
	◦	index.html
	◦	words-that-think-the-adventures-of-tom-sawyer-data.json
	2	Place them in the same folder on your computer
	3	Double-click index.html to open in your browser
	4	No internet connection required!
🎯 How to Use
	1	Navigate – Use the arrow buttons to move between groups and words
	2	Star Words – Click the ☆ button to save words to your "Starred" list
	3	Toggle Sentences – Switch between book quotes and AI examples
	4	Spelling Practice – Try the "Spell It" tab to type missing words
	5	Write Your Own – Add personal sentences in the "My Sentences" section
	6	Take Quizzes – Open the Group Quiz or Starred Words Quiz to test yourself

📖 About the Book
The Adventures of Tom Sawyer (1876) is Mark Twain's classic novel about a young boy growing up along the Mississippi River. The book is famous for its vivid characters, humorous episodes, and rich, authentic American language – making it an excellent source for vocabulary learning.
